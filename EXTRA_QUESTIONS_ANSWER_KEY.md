## Full questions with answers and notes

## Element 1: Regulatory framework

### 1. (EXTRA_001)

**Q:** What best describes the role of the Canadian Securities Administrators (CSA)?

    **A.** A federal regulator with binding national authority over all securities

✅ **B.** An umbrella body that coordinates the work of provincial and territorial securities regulators

    **C.** A self-regulatory organization that supervises Investment Dealers directly

    **D.** A division of CIRO responsible for issuing rules

**Answer: B**

*CSA is an umbrella body coordinating provincial/territorial regulators; not a federal regulator itself.*

---

### 2. (EXTRA_002)

**Q:** National Instruments (NIs) are issued by which body?

    **A.** Bank of Canada

    **B.** Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions

✅ **C.** Canadian Securities Administrators

    **D.** CIRO

**Answer: C**

*National Instruments are issued by the CSA and harmonized across provinces.*

---

### 3. (EXTRA_003)

**Q:** An Investment Dealer must register with provincial securities regulators and additionally be approved by which organization?

    **A.** FINTRAC

    **B.** OSFI

✅ **C.** CIRO

    **D.** OBSI

**Answer: C**

*Investment Dealers must be CIRO members AND register with provincial regulators.*

---

### 4. (EXTRA_004)

**Q:** Which body's primary role is to oversee federally regulated banks and ensure their financial soundness?

    **A.** FINTRAC

✅ **B.** Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI)

    **C.** CIRO

    **D.** Financial Services Regulatory Authority of Ontario (FSRA)

**Answer: B**

*OSFI supervises federally regulated banks, insurers, and pension plans for soundness.*

---

### 5. (EXTRA_005)

**Q:** The Canadian Derivatives Clearing Corporation (CDCC) primarily provides clearing services for:

    **A.** Listed equities only

    **B.** Government bond auctions

✅ **C.** Derivatives traded on Canadian exchanges

    **D.** Cryptocurrency transactions

**Answer: C**

*CDCC clears Canadian exchange-traded derivatives; CDS clears equities and fixed income.*

---

### 6. (EXTRA_006)

**Q:** Which legislation requires Investment Dealers to file Suspicious Transaction Reports?

    **A.** Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)

✅ **B.** Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorism Financing Act (PCMLTFA)

    **C.** Bank Act

    **D.** Canadian Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL)

**Answer: B**

*PCMLTFA + FINTRAC regulations require STRs for transactions suspected to be linked to money laundering.*

---

### 7. (EXTRA_007)

**Q:** The three stages of money laundering are most commonly described as:

✅ **A.** Placement, layering and integration

    **B.** Origination, distribution and concealment

    **C.** Concealment, transformation and use

    **D.** Concealment, integration and release

**Answer: A**

*Three classic stages: Placement (entering the system) -> Layering (obscuring) -> Integration (legitimate use).*

*IMET teams are multi-agency RCMP units focused on capital markets crime.*

## Element 3: Scope of client relationship

### 22. (EXTRA_022)

**Q:** Which of the following best distinguishes a discretionary account from a managed account?

    **A.** Discretionary accounts are typically used for ongoing portfolio management; managed accounts are used in temporary or unforeseen circumstances

✅ **B.** Discretionary accounts are typically used in temporary or unforeseen circumstances; managed accounts are used for ongoing portfolio management

    **C.** Discretionary accounts are reserved for institutional clients only

    **D.** Discretionary accounts must use only fee-based compensation

**Answer: B**

*Managed = ongoing portfolio management. Discretionary = typically temporary/specific authority for unforeseen circumstances.*

---

### 23. (EXTRA_023)

**Q:** The know-your-product (KYP) obligation requires the Investment Dealer to understand:

    **A.** Only the highest-risk products on the firm's shelf

✅ **B.** All investments purchased, sold or recommended for clients

    **C.** Only mutual funds and ETFs

    **D.** Only products that pay embedded commissions

**Answer: B**

*KYP applies to ALL investments purchased, sold, or recommended for clients.*

---

### 24. (EXTRA_024)

**Q:** In an order-execution-only (OEO) account, the dealer is generally NOT required to:

    **A.** Confirm the client's identity

    **B.** Accept the client's order

✅ **C.** Determine the suitability of trades for the client

    **D.** Report executed trades to the client

**Answer: C**

*OEO accounts are exempt from suitability — clients direct their own trades.*

---

### 25. (EXTRA_025)

**Q:** Performing suitability determination at the portfolio level means assessing:

    **A.** Each trade independently against the client's profile

✅ **B.** The client's overall portfolio against their objectives, risk profile and time horizon

    **C.** Only fee-based accounts

    **D.** Only institutional clients

**Answer: B**

*Portfolio-level suitability evaluates the whole portfolio against the client's profile, not just isolated trades.*

---

### 26. (EXTRA_026)

**Answer: A**

*CIRO's suitability rule for retail clients requires putting the client's interest first.*

---

### 27. (EXTRA_027)

*Account appropriateness focuses on whether the account TYPE suits the client; suitability is about specific investments.*
